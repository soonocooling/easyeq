EasyEq User Guide

EasyEq is a menu bar app that turns math written in plain Korean or English into Unicode math. Example: sqrt 2 times sqrt 3 → √2 × √3

Getting started Modes Keys Settings Writing math Troubleshooting

Getting started

  1. Open EasyEq.app and the Get started window appears. Click Open System Settings (시스템 설정 열기) and turn on EasyEq under Privacy & Security → Accessibility. The window updates as soon as permission is granted; no restart is needed.
  2. The menu bar icon shows the current state.
  3. Click the icon for on/off, modes, output format, settings and this guide.

Modes

Each mode can be turned on or off from the menu or in Settings, and both can be on at once.

Show candidates on drag

Select math in text you have already written, by dragging or double-clicking, and a candidate list appears right above the selection.

  • Click a candidate or press 1–5 to replace the selection with it.
  • Use ↑ ↓ to move and Enter to pick. The first candidate starts highlighted.
  • Press Tab to switch every candidate between Unicode and LaTeX.
  • Press Esc or 0 to keep the original text. Clicking outside or pressing any other key also closes the list, and that key still reaches your app.
  • Selecting ordinary text or empty space shows nothing.
  • By default the list appears only when you select text in an editable field (a chat box, a document, a text field). Read-only text, such as a web page or a message you received, shows nothing. Turn off 입력 칸에서 드래그할 때만 (Only in editable fields) in Settings to change this.

Show candidates while typing (live)

While you type, EasyEq watches the text just before the cursor. When it looks like math, a candidate list appears right below the text you are writing. It follows your text and updates as you type, and disappears once you move on to ordinary text.

  • Click a candidate or press ⌥ Option+1–5 to replace only the math you just typed. The rest of the sentence stays as it is.
  • Plain digits would clash with typing numbers, so live mode uses ⌥ with the digit.
  • Press Esc to close the list. It stays closed until you change that expression. The Esc key still reaches your app.
  • The quoted line at the top shows exactly what will be replaced. If you type so sqrt 2 times sqrt 3, only sqrt 2 times sqrt 3 is replaced.
  • Some apps do not expose the text being typed. The list does not appear there; select and drag instead.

If an app does not report where its text is on screen, the list appears just below the input field or near the mouse pointer.

Convert the selection now (⌃⌥E)

In apps where selection is not detected automatically, select the text and press ⌃⌥E. This shortcut also works on read-only text; there, the chosen candidate is copied to the clipboard instead of pasted, and a short “복사됨” (Copied) note appears.

Writing math

Spacing is flexible: sqrt2 times sqrt3 and sqrt 2 times sqrt 3 both work. Upper- and lowercase letters are both recognized. Korean phrases work too; see the Korean guide for the full Korean vocabulary.

  • Only the single character after _ or sub becomes a subscript. Wrap longer subscripts in braces or parentheses: x_ij → xᵢj, x_{ij} → xᵢⱼ
  • A number written right after a letter (x1) is offered as a subscript. Numbers after a multi-letter word (mp3) are left alone, and so are a lone capital letter plus number (A4, F1), which are usually ordinary words. Inside an expression such as A1 + A2, capitals are converted too.
  • With a single digit from 2 to 9, such as x2, both the subscript (x₂) and the power (x²) are offered. The subscript comes first.
  • Characters that have no Unicode subscript form (b, c, d, f, g, q, w, y, z and all capitals) stay as written, e.g. x_b.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
Nothing happensIf the menu bar icon is a warning (⚠︎), grant Accessibility permission. If it is a pause (⏸), EasyEq is off; press ⌃⌥M. If a newly built copy stops responding, remove EasyEq from the Accessibility list and add it again.
No candidates for an expressionYou may have selected read-only text; press ⌃⌥E instead. The expression may also contain a word EasyEq does not know; try the forms in the tables above. Selections over 200 characters or spanning several lines are ignored.

Privacy

Everything runs on this Mac. The log (~/Library/Logs/EasyEq.log) records only the app name, how text was read, its length and the number of candidates — never the text you select or type. Replacing text briefly uses the clipboard, and the original clipboard contents are restored 0.2 seconds later.
